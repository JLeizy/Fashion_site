# Loro Piana research notes (verified via WebSearch, 2026-11-07)
- Founded 1924 in Quarona (Piedmont, Valsesia) by engineer Pietro Loro Piana; date 4 Apr 1924 (one) / 2 Apr (another). Family wool merchants from early 19th c.; ancestor Giacomo Loro Piana woollen merchant in Trivero (one; LVMH-era press: "great-great-grandchildren of founder Giacomo") vs Giovanni (another); moved to Valsesia, founded "Lanificio F.lli Lora e Compagnia" (sp?) second half 19th c.
- Franco (Pietro's nephew) took over 1941 (one says led from 1960s); pushed exports. Sons Sergio & Pier Luigi joint CEOs from 1970s: Pier Luigi rare materials, Sergio international retail; diversification into finished goods 1980s (some 1970s).
- Vicuña: 1994 agreement with Peruvian government & Andean communities — consortium headed by Loro Piana (International Vicuña Consortium; Zegna & Agnona co-founders per one) exclusive rights to buy/process/distribute fibre; Lucanas community first to shear in 1994, sold to LP since; Pampa Galeras reserve (est. 1967) in Lucanas province; 2024 Bloomberg investigation: Lucanas doesn't receive fair price (LP disputes); 2008 private reserve "Franco Loro Piana" ~2,000 ha, Peru's first private nature reserve; 2013 majority share of firm with permission to shear wild vicuñas in Catamarca, Argentina (~85,000 ha).
- LVMH announced 8 Jul 2013: 80% (family 20%), ~€2bn, EV €2.7bn (AMF); Sergio & Pier Luigi (co-CEOs) continue; Sergio died 19 Dec 2013 aged 65 (WWD). LVMH +5% next year (85%); end-2025 LVMH bought ~€1bn more shares, 85%→94% (Wikipedia snapshot).
- Record Bale: annual Loro Piana Record Bale award honours top merino farmers in Australia & New Zealand; "The Gift of Kings" name from Spanish royal practice of gifting pairs of merino sheep to other sovereigns (LP site). 2022 authenticity & traceability service certified by Aura Blockchain Consortium (scan label).
- Lotus: Pier Luigi discovered lotus fibre ~2010; LP imports Burmese lotus silk since 2010 for jackets; only from Inle Lake, Myanmar; fibres hand-extracted; ~20,000 stems per m² (one); jacket prices $5,600–6,500 (varies).
- Milan court 14 Jul 2025: judicial administration 1 year, preventive not punitive; commissioner Micaela Cecca; failed to monitor subcontractors; outsourced via two front companies to Chinese-owned workshops in Milan without manufacturing capacity; LP not criminally charged; triggered by Chinese worker beaten after asking back wages; LP says cut ties within 24 hours of discovering subcontractors on 20 May; order may be lifted early if compliant.
- Milan court lifted judicial administration early, Fri 17 Apr 2026 (Reuters): "virtuous approach undertaken and successfully completed"; court had said LP "negligently facilitated" exploitation; LP strengthened supplier oversight: independent external auditors, more on-site inspections. Similar measures lifted for Dior, Armani subsidiary, Valentino Bags Lab.
- Storm System introduced 1994 (not 1993): waterproofing for cashmere & wool; 3-layer: natural fibre + waterproof breathable (hydrophilic) membrane + fine jersey; wind-resistant; Rain System = water-repellent surface treatment; Green Storm System 2017.
- Damien Bertrand CEO 2021 → Louis Vuitton deputy CEO from 10 Jun 2025; Frédéric Arnault CEO from 10 Jun 2025 (announced Mar 2025, transition from 26 Mar), ex-TAG Heuer CEO from 2020, head of LVMH watches early 2024; reports to Toni Belloni (president LVMH Italy).
- Summer Walk loafer launched 2005 (LP site), reflects Sergio & Pier Luigi's passion for sailing; niche until pushed 2019 (Gentleman's Gazette); nicknamed "Billionaire's Loafer"; translucent natural-rubber sole embossed; ~$980–1,150.
- Record Bale: established by Pier Luigi 1997 (WWD) / annual prize since 2000 (Wiki); 2025 winners announced in Tokyo (Tokyo National Museum); Australia: Pyrenees Park 92 kg, 10.4 microns; NZ: Earnscleugh (first-time).
- Baby Cashmere introduced 2008 (LP heritage page); ~10 years developing spinning/weaving formula; work began in Alashan mountains, Inner Mongolia; harvested once when kid goat 3–12 months old.
- Sailing: Superyacht Regatta Porto Cervo (YC Costa Smeralda) sponsor since 2008; Caribbean Superyacht Regatta title sponsor 2012 (Virgin Gorda); Giraglia Regatta first title sponsor 2024 (73rd edition 2026); Tre Golfi Sailing Week 2026 Loro Piana Challenge Trophy; Pier Luigi (Deputy Chairman) races family yacht My Song.
- Quiet luxury: Kendall Roy's black cashmere Loro Piana baseball cap $525 (Bloomberg, Fortune; $500 Rolling Stone); LP supplied some of Kendall's wardrobe on Succession; "stealth wealth". Gwyneth Paltrow ski-trial Mar 2023 (Utah 2016 accident, sought $1) wore LP among brands.
- Centenary 2024; first-ever exhibition "If You Know, You Know: Loro Piana's Quest for Excellence", Museum of Art Pudong Shanghai 22 Mar–5 May 2025, curator Judith Clark, drew on Sergio & Luisa Loro Piana Collection and Pinacoteca di Varallo; South Coast Plaza pop-up Feb 2025.
- Revenue (Fashionbi): 2023 €1,296.2m; 2024 €1,609.5m (+24.2%).
- Bloomberg (Marcelo Rochabrún, Mar 2024): vicuña sweaters ~$9,000; Lucanas 2023 contract $280/kg; community president: didn't cover workers, some work for free; Rep. Robert Garcia letter: price per kilo fell by one-third in just over a decade; unsatisfied with response; Matthieu Garnier (CEO LP North America): report "did not fairly or accurately characterize"; LP: more supplier audits, NGO projects with up to 15 communities.
- Until 1980s exclusively fabric supplier; first RTW collection 1985 (one); 1980s brothers diversified, luxury goods division. First store: 1999 Milan (one, encyclopedia-style) vs first boutique New York East 61st St initially an office (other). Mills Roccapietra & Quarona; 10+ specialised sites; quality-control lab in Roccapietra largest of its kind.
- Franco: after war exported premium wool & cashmere fabrics to Europe, America, Japan (Wikipedia: 1960s). Brothers alternated as president every three years (one). Pier Luigi first trip to China 1986 (Lampoon, Il Sole 24 Ore) / late 80s–early 90s (his 2012 interview); ~decade convincing Mongolian & Chinese herders to set aside rare fibre.
- Pecora Nera: 1983 NZ farmer Fiona Gardner acquired five dark sheep; LP buys entire dark wool since 2003; undyed natural shades; traceability since Jan 2025; Poltrona Frau upholstery with Loro Piana Interiors.
